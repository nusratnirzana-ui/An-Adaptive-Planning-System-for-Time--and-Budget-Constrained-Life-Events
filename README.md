# An Adaptive Planning System for Time and Budget Constrained Life Events
This project proposes a web-based platform that helps individuals plan and carry out personal life events,
such as moving to a new home, hosting a gathering, organizing a road trip or arranging a farewell, within a
fixed time limit and budget. The platform converts a client's event requirements into a structured plan
consisting of a phased timeline, an estimate of required supplies and a distribution of tasks among the
people involved.
The plan is adaptive. When time is lost, a participant becomes unavailable or the client's requirements
change, the plan is recalculated for the remaining time and budget rather than left as a static checklist.
Planning and re-planning are performed by an AI planning companion, which the client interacts with
through a conversational interface. Every plan is reviewed and approved by an administrator before it is
released to the client.
Because many people prefer not to carry out every task themselves, the platform also includes a
marketplace of ready-made services. Verified service providers publish packages, such as event set-up, a
moving crew or catering and clients can hire them for the whole event or for individual tasks. The
companion can recommend suitable packages when time or effort runs short.
